From first measure to hung and finished, in four steps

01

Choose in Store

Feel real samples and see how they actually drape at our Rolleston showroom before you choose colour and weight.

02

In-Home Measure

Our technician measures your windows on-site and confirms the mounting style and track type.

03

Cut & Sewn to Fit

Every panel is cut and sewn for your exact window, with pleat ratio and drop length dialled in.

04

Fit & Final Check

We install on-site and adjust in place, checking smooth operation and a clean blackout seal.
